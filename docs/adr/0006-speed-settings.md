# Speed Settings: animation scales at 0.5x, process limit and auto-updates untouched

The script sets the window, transition and animator animation scales to 0.5x, so the TV feels snappier without menus jumping. It leaves the background process limit at its normal setting so Spotify keeps playing and casting does not drop, and leaves Play auto-updates on so Netflix and the other apps never fall too far behind to work (spec, user stories 35 to 37).
